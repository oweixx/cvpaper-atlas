The collection

Six venues. One place to explore.

This atlas brings together CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, WACV, IEEE TPAMI, and IJCV. It combines official proceedings lists with publisher-deposited bibliographic metadata.

Where the data comes from

  • DBLP SPARQL: complete indexed main-venue records, ordered authors, and researcher identifiers.
  • Computer Vision Foundation — official main-conference titles, authors, paper pages, and PDF links.
  • European Computer Vision Association — official ECCV paper lists and links.
  • Crossref — exact proceedings titles and journal ISSNs, DOI records, available abstracts, and deposited-reference citation counts.
  • OpenAlex: exact DOI matches for abstracts, citation counts, and work-time affiliations.

What the numbers mean

Conference years prioritize official listings and proceedings edition titles over bibliographic publication dates. ECCV 2024 papers published in 2025 are counted in 2024. Source-year observations are retained in downloads; see the year correction audit. Workshop tracks, CVPR Findings, front matter, and records without author lists are excluded. Historical WACV editions named “Workshop on Applications of Computer Vision” are included as the main venue.

Topics and affiliation geography

Country statistics use OpenAlex author identifiers and affiliations recorded for each work, not nationality. Multiple countries can count the same author or paper. Unknown affiliations are excluded from country counts and reported in coverage. Company headquarters and institution matching can misrepresent the actual research location. See the affiliation collection ledger.

Publication versions

Matching DOI records are merged. Official listings match only within the same venue and year, using an exact normalized title and an overlapping author name. Different DOI publications and journal extensions remain separate.

Reproducibility

Every source query, retrieval date, expected count, and retained count is available in the coverage ledger. Discovery is not proof of complete historical coverage. DBLP search API access was challenged; its official SPARQL service supplied the historical records instead. Semantic Scholar was rate limited and is not claimed as a source for this snapshot.
